# Assassins-Creed-Blade-Design
Hidden blade from assassin's creed made readily (mostly) 3D printable. Ever want a cool cosplay item to show off to your friends? Make way for this sleek blade that shoots out from under your sleeve. Relatively simple assembly and fun to use.

## A breif description of how it works

The design itself isn't as intuitive as you'd think, so I'd like to use this section to explain how to works to make assembly slightly less mysterious. The blade has 2 positions, blade in and out. The blade itself has a protrusion on the side that gets caught on 2 locks. The locks always default to an outwards position, ensuring that the blade gets snagged to one of the positions. The middle slider piece closes one of these locks, allowing the spring to release all its energy into the blade through a respective slider. This is what allows it to shoot in and out. 

The rotating piece on top converts rotational motion to linear motion and allows the middle slider to either be pushed forward or backwards to shoot the blade in or out.

A [Demo video](https://github.com/gongaluga1/Assassins-Creed-Blade-Design/blob/main/Blade_Demo.mov) is included here to show what the inner mechanism should look like.

## Assembly

The build should be assembled the same way it is in the Onshape CAD. There are some notable assembly instructions to mention however.

### Sliders L and R
![image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/872655875951689768/1543822694410092624/IMG_20260830_232006.jpg?ex=6a96446d&is=6a94f2ed&hm=a1851a26bb8b0588a90bab1b68723410b35ce795c286e0739f4ff348ccbfbd92&)
These sliders are connected by a 4mm diameter 8cm length spring. I opted for hotglue to connect these two, though superglue would likely work just as good if not better. These two pieces are the wrapped around the middle slider as shown below:

![image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/872655875951689768/1543816861609758730/IMG_5298.jpg?ex=6a963efe&is=6a94ed7e&hm=d16163beb68194996f2b276bc6d9a15aa6faa931e240ccbddc4ab0cb701f1146&)

### Tolerance
This is very important. All the parts in the CAD does NOT include tolerance. Please take this into consideration when printing the parts as they will likely not fit perfectly if not accounted for.

### Print Settings
When printing, make sure the infill is set to 20% and supports & adhesion is turned ON. Otherwise, it is not necessary to edit the other settings.

### Lubrication
There are lots of moving parts, so lube is important. The sides of the sliders that come in contact with the frame all MUST be lubricated in some way whether that be grease, dry lube or even soap (though not recommended as it won't work long term).
